Determining Walls to Draw,
1. Rotate Walls relative to player's view and offset them so player is in the middle.
2. All walls that might be visible have at least one point with a positive Y (ahead of player's view).
3. We then calculate wall planes. See below.
4. Filter out walls not visible.

Calculating Wall Planes,
Wall Left Screen X Starting Point = (Point1_X / Point1_Y) * (width_x * 0.5 / 0.66)
Wall Right Screen X Ending Point = (Point1_X / Point1_Y) * (width_x * 0.5 / 0.66)
